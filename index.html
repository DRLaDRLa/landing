What's next for the e6ai community?

Hi friends,

We’d like to start by thanking all of you for your overwhelming support and kind messages. We’ve had so many incredible and thoughtful people reach out to us asking if there’s any way to help. It’s clear that e6ai and its community has meant a lot to so many people.

Our top priority has been to find a way to continue the legacy of e6ai by offering you a website where you'll be able to continue sharing your work. Initially, that led to us pursuing something that turned out to be unsustainable. It wasn’t a good fit for us. It would also not have been what this community truly needs, which is a platform that celebrates this medium and showcases the effort and creativity that can go into creating AI art.

A new plan has been set in motion, and the pieces are coming together. Right now, we are working on a new site run entirely by the original e6ai team. A team that has the drive and experience to run a curated AI furry booru and will continue guiding and supporting our community towards creating AI art of ever-improving quality.

Thanks to everyone for being so patient with us. This will be a big undertaking, and we want to do it right. We hope to see you for the launch of floofgens.net in the not-so-distant future!
